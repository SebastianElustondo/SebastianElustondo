Quovra.

My studio, and the multi-tenant platform behind it. What you just did is the whole business model: every capability is built once and switched on per client by configuration. Bookings with Mercado Pago deposits, an online store with real stock control, review funnels, gym memberships, a site builder. The acid test for every feature: can a whole client be onboarded without writing code?

  • One Postgres database behind independent Cloudflare Workers, Astro SSR for the owner and member panels, R2 for uploads, Supabase Vault for per-tenant secrets.
  • Free-tier ceilings drove the architecture: with 5 cron triggers per account, expiring holds are computed at read time, so work scales with usage, not with tenants.
  • Deposits go straight to each business's own Mercado Pago account. Per-tenant credentials live in Supabase Vault; only the payment webhook can confirm, or undo, a paid booking.
  • Deny-all row-level security: no table is readable by any client key; every query runs server-side through the service role, scoped by tenant.

      Reachr.

      From lead to client, from client to payment. A WhatsApp-first CRM for freelancers and micro-agencies in Latin America who sell recurring services to local businesses. The part every CRM forgets is collections, and that's the part you just used. I run my own client outreach on it every day.
